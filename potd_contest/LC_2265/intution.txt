Intuition

For every node, we need to know the sum and number of nodes in its entire subtree to calculate its average.

So, for a node:

First get the (sum, count) of its left subtree.
Get the (sum, count) of its right subtree.
Add the current node:
sum = leftSum + rightSum + root->val
count = leftCount + rightCount + 1
Now the average of this subtree is sum / count.
If root->val is equal to this average, add 1 to the answer.
Return the current subtree's (sum, count) to its parent.

We use postorder traversal because the parent cannot calculate its subtree's sum/count until it has the information from both children.

Important: The average is integer division, so sum / count is enough.
